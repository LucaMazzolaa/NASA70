Founded in 1958, NASA is celebrating its 70th anniversary this year. This project aims to highlight the agency’s vast archive of data, images, and documents, curated specifically for the historic year of 2028, offering a collection of interactive experiences that retrace and interpret more than half a century of space exploration, spanning milestones from the first space missions to contemporary exploration efforts, across generations of innovation and discovery. Through these contents, the archive becomes a space for exploration and storytelling, where the memory of missions and space discoveries is reorganized and reinterpreted in a contemporary way, connecting the past, present, and future.
Each contribution reflects a personal interpretation of how to celebrate NASA’s 70 years, combining research, selection, and visual storytelling to reimagine space exploration through interactive digital experiences and digital narratives. By transforming data, images, and documents into engaging visual formats, each project proposes a distinct way of navigating, understanding, and exploring the content.
These projects have been developed by students from different schools, working with publicly available NASA data and materials online. To ensure a centralized and consistent architecture, a dedicated API was used as the sole data source. The API structure itself was designed and developed by a university class. Consequently, all page elements such as titles, metadata, and media files are pulled from a single open-source GitHub repository.
