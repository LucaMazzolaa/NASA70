Founded in 1958, NASA is celebrating its 70th anniversary this year. This project aims to highlight the agency’s vast archive of data, images, and documents, offering a collection of interactive experiences that retrace and interpret more than half a century of space exploration. Through these contents, the archive becomes a space for exploration and storytelling, where the memory of missions and space discoveries is reorganized and reinterpreted in a contemporary way, connecting the past, present, and future of aerospace research.
© 2026
These projects have been developed by students from different schools, working with publicly available NASA data and materials online.
Each contribution reflects a personal interpretation of how to celebrate NASA’s 70 years, combining research, selection, and visual storytelling to reimagine space exploration through interactive digital experiences and digital narratives. The API was used as the sole data source.
